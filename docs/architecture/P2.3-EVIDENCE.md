## Nullable Relationships

A synthetic valid model confirms extraction does not fail when:

- A Space has no BuildingStory.
- A Space has no ThermalZone.
- A Surface has no parent Space or PlanarSurfaceGroup.
- A SubSurface has no parent Surface.

Missing references serialize as `null` facts. P2.3 does not turn them into findings; that remains P3.

## Coordinate Frames

- Geometry assigned to a Space uses `coordinate_system = "space_local"`.
- Orphan Surface/SubSurface geometry uses `coordinate_system = "model"`.
- The inventory never performs implicit site-coordinate conversion.

Both coordinate modes are backed by synthetic OpenStudio fixtures.
